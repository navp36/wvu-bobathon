    How it protects student information

    This prototype collects nothing.
    • Everything runs in your browser. No server, no network calls, no analytics.
    • Your selections are not saved: reload and they are gone (no cookies, no localStorage).
    • The catalog is fictional. No real student records are used.
    • A production version would need single sign-on, read-only scoped access, and no storage of grades or IDs outside the registrar's systems.
